# Example sessions

Illustrations of the blocks. They are not a script to recite. During practice, do not open this file.

The cards, bars, and summary below are renderer output, so the columns match a real session.

## idk

User: idk

```
? goodhart's law.
  When the count became the bonus, the team
  started producing the count. Rename the
  bonus around bugs that stay fixed.
```

User: 2. I'm copying the visible wins and ignoring the people who vanished.

✓ survivorship bias: strong pick.
  The folded companies are the quiet part of
